Where to look when nobody tells you.

Half of this job is knowing where to search. These are the references we use every day, and the ones our labs expect you to find on your own.

Industry foundations

Free, community-built and used across the industry.

  • The Twelve-Factor App↗

    The twelve rules for building apps that survive production. The starting point for everyone.

  • Google SRE Books↗

    Free and complete: monitoring, SLOs, on-call and how to not burn out your team.

  • CNCF Projects↗

    The cloud native ecosystem: Kubernetes, Prometheus and everything your stack probably already uses.

  • Open Containers Initiative↗

    The open standard for container images and runtimes. What “runs anywhere” actually means.

  • Play with Docker↗

    A scratch playground for containers. Good for warming up before our labs.

References you’ll keep open

  • Public Postmortems↗

    A huge collection of outages and incidents, documented by the people who lived them.

  • Learning from Incidents↗

    Public postmortems from major platforms. Reading how others failed is the cheapest way to learn.

  • Cloud Docs↗

    Your cloud provider’s official docs. Long, sometimes confusing, but the source of truth.

  • End of Life Calendar↗

    Changelogs and release notes for your tools. What breaks a deploy is rarely a mystery: it is written down.
