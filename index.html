Pick Your Poison

The pipeline is red. The clock is ticking.

Where do you start?

  • CI/CD Without Theater

    Pipelines that run where they should: fast, reliable and boring. No ten-approval chains nobody reads.

  • Infrastructure as Code

    If your infrastructure is not versioned, it does not exist. Terraform with real review and plans that do not surprise anyone.

  • Kubernetes Under Fire

    Manifests you still understand in December. Probes, limits and rollbacks that do not require a ritual.

  • Observability That Answers

    Metrics, logs and traces that reply before your client does. Alerts that do not scream about everything.

  • Friday Deploy Drills

    Feature flags, gradual rollouts and rollback plans that are not a wish. Deploying on Friday stops being a bet.

  • Incident Forensics

    Rebuild what happened from incomplete logs and a confusing Slack thread. No invented villains.

  • Backups That Restore

    A backup without a restore test is a wish. Automate actually checking it, not just dreaming about it.

  • Honest Automation

    Automate what truly repeats. Document the rest and keep living. Scripts nobody understands are debt too.

  • Cost and Capacity

    The cloud is not free and autoscaling is not a budget strategy. Read the bill before finance does.

  • Controlled Game Days

    We break things on purpose, with permission and an exit plan. Controlled friction builds muscle.
